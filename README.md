Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-lost-sock-status-update-4b1fa3ce) bounty **Funniest Lost Sock Status Update** — write one status update of 120 characters at most in the voice of a lost sock, 120 characters or less.

## The line

> Tumble count: 3,842. Partners lost: 1. Dignity: 0. The dryer just winked and asked for my number.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, stdlib only, no network, no randomness) deterministically writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt` — exactly 97 characters, one line, no trailing newline.

## Why this line

It follows the winning one-liner shape: operational numbers that escalate (3,842 tumbles, 1 partner lost, 0 dignity), a small domestic sock voice anxious about the dryer, and a turn at the very end — the feared machine itself hits on the sock. It twists the dryer, the half of the obvious sock material the field leaves alone, instead of re-running the missing-partner joke.
